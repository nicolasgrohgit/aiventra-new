Odys.ai

Delete your account

Odys.ai is published by Nicolas Groh. This page explains how to delete your Odys.ai account and the data stored with it.

Fastest: delete it in the app

  1. Open Odys.ai and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. The account and its data are deleted immediately.

No longer have the app?

We handle requests by hand, within 30 days. Because anyone can type any address here, we confirm with the owner of the address before deleting anything.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: email address, name, profile photo, home base, preferences, and notification token.
  • Your trips, and the events you created or imported from your calendar for them.
  • Your circles: the contacts you added, and friend requests you sent or received.
  • Your place in other people's data: you are removed from their contact lists, from events they invited you to, and trips you shared stop appearing for them.
  • Feedback you sent us through the app.

What is not deleted

  • A Google Play subscription is not cancelled. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, otherwise Google keeps charging you.
  • Purchase records held by Google Play and by RevenueCat, our billing provider. They are tied to a random account identifier, not to your name or email.
  • Server request logs, which are removed automatically after 30 days.
  • Data stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes it.
